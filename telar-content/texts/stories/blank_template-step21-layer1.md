     The Codex Mendoza offers tremendous possibilities, from offering an idea of Nahua cultural practices, as well as Aztec imperial conquest, and even a scope into comprehending Spanish understandings of such pictorial manuscripts and limitations of translation.  Importantly, the main possibility the Codex Mendoza holistically offers is a general understanding of Nahua and Aztec culture!  Demonstrating daily life of the Nahua, the Codex allows a cultural remembrance that can be shared with people even outside of their culture – for them to gain an understanding of such culture.  For example, analyzing 57r you can gain perspective into the cultural traditions surrounding child birth, such as midwifery and priestly cooperation.  Furthermore, the world can also gain insight into a literal visibly and visceral retailing of history, allowing one to further imagine what the time may have genuinely looked like, even without having knowledge of the genuine oral tradition – which demonstrates the conclusive literacy of pictorial writing.  Overall, the possibilities of the Codex Mendoza lie in its significant cultural remembrance, and the possibility for such remembrance to be shared with those outside of the culture of which it was borne.
