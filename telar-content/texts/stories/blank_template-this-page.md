In the first half of this page, Rodriguez is taching about painting spaces, silences, elipsis, braces, and hyphen while also explaining their role and importance in his writing philosophy. 

Then he breaks from this explanation to note how for this type of writing to work, one must be able to feel. At the same time, he asserted that there was no possible way to learn how to feel.

He emphasizes instead the need to learn how to express someone else's emotions, and the way these emotions affect personal ones.
