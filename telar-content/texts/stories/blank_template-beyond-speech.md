---
title: "Beyond Speech"
---

Speech has been an eseential part of politics and governance. Plato's Republic and Dialogues are an important landmark of this art.

Rodriguez however was more concerned about certain parallels between old ways of governance and language.

He came to the realization that both government and language were in need of reform while having a long shared history of reformers that tried to execute change.

This page and the following pages in the Prodromus are a testament of this dynamic speech-print-government connection. Rodriguez had the goal to ultimately reform the customs in which both Language and Government were founded.
