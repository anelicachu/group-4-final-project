---
title: "The need to invent."
---

Rodriguez understood something profound about speech and governments that was ahead of his time. 

He understood first that a the Spanish America must invent its own unique system of governance because no other system could represent the diverse, difficult, and struggling history of the New World. 

With this idea, he invented a new way of paiting thoughts and presenting ideas, proper to new countries. 

From the Mexica elite using codexes to save their culture, to the Incas appropiating and using it to its full potential. When faced with change beyond one's control, one must invent. 

After the invention of print, in need of standardizing language, one must invent. 

Rodriguez understood the shortcomings of old systems and he was in favor of fundamental change that tended to the realities of the society of his time. If one does not invent, one fails.
