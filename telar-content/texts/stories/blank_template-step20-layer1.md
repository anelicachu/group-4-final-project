title: "Learn more"
---

     An obvious limitation of the Codex Mendoza, or any Mexican Codex, is the universality of understanding.  As such rely on genuine oral tradition and communal understanding, the general Spanish population – that colonized the region – would not be able to comprehend the manuscripts.  Therefore, translation became necessary; hence, one can view annotations on the pages of the codex, as well as commentary on the side, all clearly phonetic.  It is noted by the translator that the commentary was done in quite a rush.  In fact, such was detailed to be done in ten days, and with such came various translational and linguistic mistakes, so much so that the translator makes a note to apologize for such on 71v.  Specifically, the translator apologizes for a 'rough' interpretation of the pictorially enunciated history, as well as the use of words of Moorish derivation.  Overall, the limitations of the Codex Mendoza specifically regard to its universality, especially with regard to specific translation.
