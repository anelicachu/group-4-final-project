---
title: "The fundamental view."
---

In page 39 of Sociedades Americanas, Rodriguez mentions two main components (or rules) that the art of writing must follow.

First it is paint symbols that were liked the movements of our mouth, a loose way of saying what had been an important movement in the castellano language: To write as you speak. To have a written register equivalent to the sounds that we use on a day to day basis.

The second principle was to paint thoughts in the way in which they are conceived. This principle applied specifically to the school of print that Rodriguez had been developing. Rodriguez thought that print, speech, writing, and feeling were things that were connected at a fundamental level and could not be treated separately. 

These two principles are part of a bigger effort to desmonstrate this connection, and form part of Rodriguez' fundamental philosophy that shapes his intricate way of writing. As his writing is divided to mimic speech, and bravely challenge the notion that words "must be in a line like pearls on a thread".








> 
  Rodríguez, S. (1990). Sociedades americanas. Biblioteca Ayacucho.
