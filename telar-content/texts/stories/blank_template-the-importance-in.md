---
title: "The Importance in Inca Culture "
---

The quipu differs from the many different record keeping methods seen around the world. The quipu seems as if it is not the most conventional method of writing and record keeping, but it was embedded into their culture and their way of life. Quipus are a representation of how writing systems are a cultural norm and social agreement. Each detail represented in a quipu, from the way the knots face, to their colors and lengths was agreed upon by their society. As mentioned before by Garcilaso, it was passed down from father to son through many generations and towns had multiple quipucamayocs. Passing the knowledge along would help keep the tradition and social agreement alive. To this day, there is no way to decipher a quipu,  there have been many guesses, but with the way the Inca developed the language, it helps with anonymity.

Garcilaso - Posts, relays, and the quipus (Royal Commentaries bk 6 chs 7-9, pp. 328-333)
